Dawes Arboretum

Building a better garden border.

At Dawes Arboretum, our crew spent the day digging out flower beds and making a new border for one of them. We used a level to keep the trench and border boards at a clean, even edge, and we had to stay aware of pipes and underground utilities the whole time we were digging.

It was a small taste of real site work: laying out the border, thinking about grading so water drains away from the bed instead of pooling, and making sure everything lined up before anything went in the ground.
